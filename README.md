# 🚗 Car Rental Sales & Profit Analysis Dashboard (Power BI)

An interactive Power BI dashboard that analyzes car rental bookings, sales and profit across cities, car types, fuel types, booking channels and customer types.

## 🎯 Objective
To understand which cities, car types and booking channels bring in the most sales and profit, how bookings change month by month, and how many booking requests are accepted.

## 🧩 Dashboard Features
- **KPI cards:** total sales, total profit, profit margin %, total bookings, accepted bookings and accepted rate %
- **Line chart:** sales and profit by booking month
- **Column charts:** sales and profit by car type, plus sales, profit and weekend bookings
- **Bar charts:** sales and profit by fuel type and by booking channel
- **Donut chart:** sales split by customer type (New vs Returning)
- **City slicer:** filters the whole dashboard by the city a booking starts from (From_City)
